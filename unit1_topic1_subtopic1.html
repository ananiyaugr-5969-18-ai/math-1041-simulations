The Vector Space Axioms

Section 4.1.1: Foundations of Linear Algebra

Addition Rules
(1) Closure

Adding two vectors yields a vector in the same space.

\( \mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v} \in V \)
(2) Commutativity

Order of addition doesn't matter.

\( \mathbf{u} + \mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v} + \mathbf{u} \)
(4) Zero Element

Existence of an additive identity.

\( \mathbf{u} + \mathbf{0} = \mathbf{u} \)
